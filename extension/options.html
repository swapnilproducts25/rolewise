Rolewise Fill settings

To draft answers right inside job forms ("✨ Draft" under a question), Rolewise Fill calls Claude with your own Anthropic API key. The key is stored only in this browser, and each draft sends the question, the job description excerpt and your CV facts to Anthropic. A drafted answer usually costs well under one cent.

  1. Open console.anthropic.com, sign in, and add a small amount of credit under Billing.
  2. Go to API keys, create a key, and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and click Save.
